# Pixora 📸

### Your Photos. Your Privacy. Your Gallery.

Pixora is a modern Android photo and video gallery designed to give users a fast, beautiful, and private way to browse, organize, view, and manage their media.

## Features

- **Fast & Responsive**: Built with performance in mind for large libraries.
- **Ad-free**: No advertisements, ever.
- **Privacy-focused**: No hidden analytics, no mandatory accounts, no automatic cloud uploads. Your photos stay on your device.
- **Offline-first**: Works entirely without an internet connection.
- **Open source**: Fully open-source under the MIT license.

## Tech Stack

- React Native
- Expo
- TypeScript
- Android MediaStore
